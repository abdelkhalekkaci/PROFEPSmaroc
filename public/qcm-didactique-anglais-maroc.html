💡 Justification didactique : Information gap, choice, and feedback (Morrow, 1981) are the three defining features of truly communicative classroom activities.

In an EFL lesson, what does instructional 'Scaffolding' mean within Vygotsky's Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD)?

Q90 Minimal Pairs in Pronunciation Remediation Teaching Pronunciation

C) To practice skimming a five-page newspaper article

💡 Justification didactique : Targeted minimal-pair discrimination (listening first, then controlled production) sharpens phonological awareness of contrasts absent in the learners' L1.
Q91 Cohesion vs Coherence in Writing Instruction Discourse Competence & Writing

How should a teacher explain the difference between 'Coherence' and 'Cohesion' when training Baccalaureate students in essay writing?

A) Coherence is the logical flow and unified progression of ideas at the macro-level, whereas cohesion is the surface linguistic linking of sentences through connectors, pronouns, and lexical chains ✓ Réponse officielle

C) Adding 'Moreover' and 'However' at the start of random, unrelated sentences guarantees perfect coherence
D) Coherence only applies to poetry and cohesion only applies to informal emails
💡 Justification didactique : Students often overuse mechanical linking words ('Moreover, Furthermore') without logical coherence; good writing requires both conceptual unity (coherence) and accurate referencing/transitioning (cohesion).
Q92 Dictogloss Technique (Ruth Wajnryb) Integrated Skills & Grammar

How does the 'Dictogloss' (grammar dictation) technique integrate listening, note-taking, collaborative reconstruction, and grammar awareness?

D) Students memorize a poem at home and recite it individually
💡 Justification didactique : Dictogloss pushes learners to notice grammatical and cohesive gaps as they negotiate how to turn isolated content words back into a coherent, grammatical text.

What is the contemporary didactic consensus regarding the use of the learners' mother tongue (Arabic/Amazigh) in the Moroccan EFL classroom?
